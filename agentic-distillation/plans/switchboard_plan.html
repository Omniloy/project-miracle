What PhoneLLM is, and what is actually open

PhoneLLM Alpha 1 (pipecat-ai/phonellm-alpha-1, HF 2026-08-24, blog 2026-08-27) is a full-parameter bf16 SFT (NVIDIA NeMo) of Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B (hybrid Mamba-2 + attention + MoE, 3.5B active), English-only, trained and served thinking-OFF at temperature 0, exposed as a plain OpenAI-compatible vLLM endpoint (qwen3_coder XML tool parser, nano_v3 reasoning-parser plugin, --default-chat-template-kwargs enable_thinking=false). Training data is proprietary ('real-world telephone and customer support' scenarios, synthetic + curated production traces; the only quantitative hint is a 1,000-row NVFP4 calibration set spanning 273 conversations / 692 strata).

Evaluated on PhoneBench Alpha 1 (15 models, human-calibrated LLM-judge panel, seven axes: telephone speaking style, tool-call accuracy, say/do consistency, factual grounding, coherence, authentication & escalation discipline, caller outcome; harness NOT public): Gemini 3.6 Flash 78.6, GPT-5.6 Terra 72.4, PhoneLLM 72.3, untuned Qwen3.8-27B 70.0, Claude Sonnet 5 68.9, Nemotron base 28.6.

Serving: modeled $0.0025/agent-minute on Modal B200s at 44 concurrent LLM processes (88 agents by an unexplained sweep construction), sub-600 ms P95 TTFAT; the official NVFP4 checkpoint was made AFTER training by ModelOpt 0.46.0rc1 selective PTQ (lm_head, sensitive attention/Mamba projections and conv1d unquantized) with in-domain calibration, costing -0.04 PhoneBench points (bf16 KV) or -0.54 (FP8 KV).

What this means for us

  • our base already scores 70.0 vs PhoneLLM's 72.3, so headroom is a few points on style/say-do/escalation, not a gross jump
  • Pipecat's own voice-readiness board shows Qwen3.8-27B NVFP4 (unsloth checkpoint, SGLang, no MTP) at 97.8% / 101 ms P50 on one RTX 5090 - independent validation of our base
  • Pipecat did NOT fine-tune a quantized model; they trained bf16 then PTQ'd
  • their reference prompt FORBIDS narrating tool use, contradicting our 'acknowledge before tool' idea, so narration must be prompt-conditional
  • vLLM prefix caching is a no-op for nemotron_h hybrids on GPU (issue #54504) - a concrete serving weakness we can beat if Qwen3.8's GDN path caches correctly

Replicate

  • thinking-off, temperature-0 SFT of an open hybrid base for English phone customer-service tool use; strict train/serve alignment baked into the vLLM launch (--default-chat-template-kwargs '{"enable_thinking":false}' --override-generation-config '{"temperature":0}'); say/do discipline as the headline trained behaviour.
  • bf16 training first, THEN ModelOpt selective NVFP4 PTQ calibrated on ~1,000 in-domain, eval-disjoint rows stratified over the taxonomy, with lm_head / GDN conv1d / in_proj_a,b / MTP head excluded; publish bf16-vs-NVFP4 delta per eval axis, and bf16-KV vs FP8-KV.
  • a PhoneBench-shaped held-out eval (seven axes, reference-response comparison, human-calibrated judge panel) plus TTFAT and modeled $/agent-minute using Pipecat's convention (GPU $/hr / utilisation / concurrent agents under P95 target).
  • Pipecat integration slot - vLLM OpenAI-compatible server + OpenAILLMService(base_url=...) reusing pipecat-examples/phonellm (swap model id) and its pipecat-eval naturalness.yaml/reservations.yaml as a smoke suite.

Improve

  • bilingual EN + ES phone traces (PhoneLLM is English-only; Qwen3.8 base is Apache-2.0 and multilingual) - a real product differentiator with a measurable eval split.
  • prompt-conditional narration style - the model obeys either 'acknowledge + set expectation before slow/async tools' or Pipecat's 'never narrate, call then report', selected by the system prompt; PhoneLLM's example prompt only supports the latter.
  • explicit long-conversation robustness (20-30+ turns, 3+ tool calls, slot changes mid-call) with a dedicated eval split, addressing the 'falls off a cliff at turn 4-5' criticism of thinking-off models.
  • ASR-noise-aware behaviour (digit/name read-back, targeted repair, no write-tool with an unconfirmed misheard value) trained with synthetic ASR corruption of user turns - SpeechGym shows perceptual slot mishearing dominates real voice failures.
  • calibrated escalation - train both 'transfer when warranted' and 'refuse unnecessary transfer' (our first adapter over-transferred; PhoneBench penalises poor escalation discipline).
  • a published, reproducible eval harness (fork kwindla/aiewf-eval, add PhoneBench axes + taxonomy probes + Spanish + long-call splits) since PhoneBench's harness is withheld.
  • serving - verify and exploit working prefix caching for Qwen3.8's GDN layers (--mamba-cache-mode align) plus MTP k=2 on a single RTX PRO 6000, reporting P95 TTFAT concurrency instead of tok/s; PhoneLLM/nemotron_h re-prefills every turn on GPU.

Do not chase

  • cost per agent-minute. A dense 27B has ~8x the active compute of Nemotron Nano; our single-GPU $/min will be several times PhoneLLM's $0.0025. Lead with quality-per-active-parameter, bilinguality and latency, not cost.

The NVFP4 decision

Decision. Do not fine-tune a pre-quantized community NVFP4 checkpoint. Train in bf16 with a larger adapter than v0, merge, then quantize selectively with the MTP head and the delta-sensitive layers protected. Keep two fallbacks alive from day one.

Why not train on a quantized checkpoint directly: no official Qwen or NVIDIA NVFP4 of this model exists, only community ones; PhoneLLM itself was trained in bf16 and quantized afterwards; full-model quantization-aware training of a 27B needs several 80 GB GPUs; and our v0 adapter's delta (about 0.3% of weight magnitude) already fell below FP8's precision, so a four-bit grid with 25 to 50% relative steps would erase a small delta entirely. That last point is inference from the format, not a measurement; the KL and eval gates are the actual test.

Identifier capture and validation

Added after review: callers hand over DNI, NIE, passport and phone numbers in dozens of spoken shapes, with dots, in pairs or hundreds, "doble cinco", "efe de Francia", "cero" versus "o", and telephony ASR mishears them in predictable ways. This family fixes what the agent must do at each step: name the document type, normalize the spoken form to the canonical string, validate the checksum where one exists (DNI and NIE mod 23, CIF mod 10, IBAN mod 97, card Luhn), read back in chunks, re-ask exactly one failing segment on a bounded budget, and never call a write tool with an unvalidated value. The 20 patterns sit in the taxonomy below; this is the reference table they lean on.

Open design decision. The research recommends that a passing checksum replaces the confirmation turn for lookups (Google conversation-design practice: explicit confirmation only for high-cost steps), while the phase-1 authoring brief requires a chunked read-back of every identifier before any write. Phase 1 keeps read-back always for consistency; phase 2 should make it per identifier: implicit for checksummable values used only in lookups, explicit for anything that feeds a write tool or has no checksum.

The pattern taxonomy

Every pattern names its trigger in terms of the tool-result shape or the conversational state, gives one utterance in the register we want, and defines a check that can score it. Open a pattern to see its example and check. Anti-patterns are rejection rules that run programmatically before any judge.

One design choice worth flagging. Pipecat's reference prompt forbids narrating tool use ("no let me check, no one moment"), while the brief for this project wants a human-like acknowledgement before a lookup. The plan trains both as prompt-conditional styles, so the same model obeys whichever the system prompt asks for. Half the dataset uses each.

Training recipe

  • Base: Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B bf16 (Apache-2.0), text-only; strip/ignore the vision tower if present in the checkpoint; keep the native MTP head untouched during SFT.
  • Method (P1): LoRA r=128, alpha=256, dropout 0.05, target ALL linear layers - attention q/k/v/o, GDN in_proj_qkv, in_proj_z, in_proj_a, in_proj_b, out_proj, MLP gate/up/down; NOT embeddings/lm_head/conv1d/norms. Rationale: a larger, all-layer delta is more likely to survive NVFP4 PTQ than our r=64 adapter; still fits one 96 GB GPU (bf16 weights ~54 GB + adapter + 8k-token activations with gradient checkpointing).
  • Hyperparameters: LR 1e-4 cosine, warmup 3%, 2 epochs over ~30M tokens, effective batch 32 sequences (micro-batch 1 x grad-accum 32), max seq 8,192 (long calls up to 16k in a second bucket), bf16, Liger/flash kernels, loss on assistant tokens only (both spoken content and tool_call JSON), user/tool/system masked; no packing across conversations.
  • Template alignment: render every sample with tokenizer.apply_chat_template(..., enable_thinking=False) so the assistant turn begins with the same empty think block vLLM will produce at inference (exact string for Qwen3.8 UNVERIFIED - diff against a vLLM render on 50 samples before training). Tool results as role=tool messages; tools passed in the tools argument, not hand-written into the system prompt.
  • Unattended Vast job: single script that pulls data from HF, trains, merges LoRA into bf16 (peft merge_and_unload), pushes merged bf16 + adapter to a private HF repo, then runs the tau3 banking dev eval (1 trial) and the PhonePatterns-Eval on the merged bf16 via vLLM 0.28.0 and pushes results JSON; checkpoint to HF every 500 steps so a preempted box loses < 1 h.
  • Gate after bf16 SFT: tau3-banking pass^1 >= base thinking-off (no regression), PhonePatterns-Eval composite >= base + 3 points with say/do >= 4.7/5 and escalation discipline >= base, Spanish split within 2 points of English. If fails: inspect judge rationales per stratum, regenerate weak strata, retrain (budget 3 iterations).
  • Quantization (P1): ModelOpt >= 0.46.0 hf_ptq on the merged bf16: MLP W4A4 NVFP4 g16 (quant_algo NVFP4, not W4A16_NVFP4); FP8 for attention q/k/v/o and GDN in_proj_qkv/z/out_proj; exclude lm_head, all *.conv1d, in_proj_a, in_proj_b, norms, embeddings, mtp.* (regex 'mtp*'); kv_cache_quant_algo none (bf16 KV) with an FP8-KV variant measured separately; calibration on the 1,024 in-domain rows. Alternative toolchain if ModelOpt's Qwen3.8 GDN handling fails: llm-compressor with the unsloth/RedHatAI compressed-tensors recipe (MLP layers 0-55 W4A4, attn/GDN FP8, ignore re:^mtp.*), noting RedHatAI's warning that this format only works in vLLM, not SGLang.
  • Quantization gate: NVFP4-merged within 0.5 pts composite of bf16-merged, within 1.0 pt on tau3 pass^1, MTP k=2 acceptance >= 0.75, KL(bf16||NVFP4) on 500 held-out turns <= 1.5x the base model's own KL under the same recipe; also check no degradation on the Spanish split (quantization can hit lower-resource tokens harder).
  • Fallback (P2) if the fine-tune does not survive PTQ: ModelOpt QLoRA - quantize.py --recipe nvfp4 (custom exclusions as above) --compress True on the base; train.py with qlora_nvfp4.yaml adapted (r=32-64, LR 2e-4, 1-2 epochs, seq 8k, DDP single GPU since FSDP2 export is unsupported); export.py; serve with --enable-lora. Pre-test: determinism/quality A/B at temperature 0 (vLLM issue #50059) before committing.
  • Fallback (P3): ship bf16 base + unmerged r=128 LoRA + MTP k=2 (already proven serving path) if neither P1 nor P2 passes gates within the timeline; NVFP4 becomes a follow-up.
  • Optional stage (only if S2 leaves >= 3,000 judge-rejected turns): one epoch of DPO/ORPO on (accepted, rejected) pairs for say/do and over-transfer, LR 5e-6, beta 0.1, after SFT and before merge; skip if SFT already hits say/do >= 4.8/5.
  • MTP head: measure acceptance with the adapter/merged weights; if < 0.75, consider a short MTP-head-only fine-tune on our data (tooling for Qwen3.8 MTP training is UNVERIFIED) or drop to k=1.
